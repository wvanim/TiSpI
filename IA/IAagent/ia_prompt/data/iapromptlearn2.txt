We add event tags:
P "root"
  F group "f0"
    P "button",
      F group "f0", MOUSE_OUT, color green, text "Next"
      F group "f1", MOUSE_HOVER, color yellow, text "Next..."
      F group "f2", MOUSE_PUSHED, color black, text "Next !"

In fact, the tag is an event sent by the Tispi engine.
